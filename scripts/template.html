“Agents are only as useful as the systems they can act on.”

Microsoft's Azure blog for Ignite 2026

That line explains a lot of this year's news. In a Forrester survey quoted by Microsoft, 91% of IT leaders said modernization is necessary for AI, but on average only 32% of their applications were AI-ready. So the prompt asks Copilot a question most recaps skip: which of your systems would an agent need to reach, and are they ready?
